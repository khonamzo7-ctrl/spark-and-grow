GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Here are a few useful details before you request a service.

Still have a question? Ask us ↗
Are the prices on the website final?

No. They are starting prices. We confirm the full scope and final quotation before work begins.

Do I need to pay to request a quotation?

No. Sending an enquiry is free, and an enquiry does not mean you have placed an order.

Can you guarantee my funding application will be approved?

No. We can help you prepare documents and organise information, but funding decisions are made by the relevant funding institution.

How long does a project take?

Delivery depends on the service and complexity of the request. We will discuss a timeframe with you before confirming work.

How do I send my enquiry?

Complete the form below, then copy your prepared message. Direct sending will be enabled once official business contact details have been added.
